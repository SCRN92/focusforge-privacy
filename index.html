FocusForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 April 2026

FocusForge ("the app") is an offline productivity app (Pomodoro timer and habit tracker). This policy explains what information the app and its third-party components handle.

1. Information the app itself collects

None. All tasks, focus sessions, habits, and settings are stored locally on your device. The app does not run its own servers, does not require an account, and does not transmit your personal productivity data to us or anyone else.

2. Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to alert you when a focus session or break ends.
  • Foreground service — to keep the timer running accurately when the app is backgrounded.
  • Internet — used only by the advertising and billing SDKs listed below.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • Approximate (non-precise) location derived from IP
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • App interaction data related to ads

This data is used by Google to serve and measure ads. You can reset or opt out of your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings. See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

User Messaging Platform (UMP) / consent

In the European Economic Area, the UK, and other regions that require it, the app presents a Google-provided consent form before loading personalised ads. Your choice is stored by Google and honoured by AdMob.

Google Play Billing

If you purchase a one-time upgrade (e.g. to remove ads), the transaction is processed by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase is valid; we do not see or store your payment details.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It is not a "family" app on Google Play.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because the app stores everything locally, uninstalling it removes all your data from the device. There is no server-side account to delete.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
